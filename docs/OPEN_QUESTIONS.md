
## Sponsored workers (S5-04, 14 Nov 2026)
- Does Michael's company hold a sponsor licence, and who is its Authorising Officer / Level 1 user? SHC tracks visas,
  CoS references and changes to report, but the reporting itself is done on the Home Office Sponsor Management System.
- The 2025 immigration changes closed the overseas care worker route to new applicants (in-country switching continued
  for a time). Michael should confirm with his immigration adviser which of his recruits can still be sponsored, and the
  current salary thresholds. SHC gives no immigration advice.
